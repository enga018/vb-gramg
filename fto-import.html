The system records status history and does not delete unrelated charges. Mixed statuses under one job card are reconciled from individual reference numbers.

6. Applicant Matching Review

FTO names are compared with applicants in the active MIS. Matches below 80%, or with less than a 10-point lead over the next candidate, require your confirmation. An applicant already attached to a charge is retained unless you explicitly select a different MIS applicant.
